Dear Guest,

Each spring we ask departing guests to rate their room, and this year one
category stood clearly below the others for comfort. Beginning September 1 we
will refurbish every room in that category: new beds, new lighting, and better
sound insulation from the corridor.

The work will take six weeks. Guests who already hold a reservation in that
category during the period will be moved to the next category up, at no
additional charge. There is no need to contact us; the change will appear on
your booking automatically.

Rates for all categories remain as published until January.

Sincerely,
Amara Boateng
General Manager
