NOTICE TO ALL TENANTS AND EXHIBITORS
Westbrook Convention Center
To: All tenants and exhibitors
From: Building Operations Office
Date: June 3

In preparation for the summer trade season, the center has updated its fire safety regulations. The rules below take effect on June 10 and apply to every rented space.

1. Corridors, stairwells, and doorway exits must be kept clear of boxes and equipment at all times.
2. Open flames, including candles and cooking burners, are prohibited inside the exhibit halls.
3. A damaged fire extinguisher or alarm pull station must be reported to the operations office in Room 118 within 24 hours.
4. Each hall's occupancy limit is listed in Appendix A and must never be exceeded.

A full evacuation drill will be held on Tuesday, June 11, at 10:00 A.M., and all tenants must take part. When the alarm sounds, stop work, leave at once by the nearest marked exit, and do not return for personal belongings. Questions may be directed to fire warden Ms. Delia Ruiz, extension 2402.

APPENDIX A — EVACUATION ROUTES BY FLOOR
Westbrook Convention Center

Read this guide together with the Fire Safety Regulations notice, and learn the route for your floor before an emergency occurs.

Floor 1 — Halls A and B: 300 persons. Primary exit: the main lobby doors. Secondary exit: the loading-dock doors at the rear. Assembly point: parking lot P2.

Floor 2 — Hall C: 450 persons. Primary exit: Stairwell C, beside the north café. Secondary exit: Stairwell D. Assembly point: Fountain Plaza on Elm Street.

Floor 3 — Halls D and E: 520 persons. Primary exit: Stairwell B, at the east end of the corridor. Secondary exit: Stairwell A. Assembly point: Fountain Plaza on Elm Street.

Floor 4 — Meeting rooms 401–410: 610 persons for the entire floor. Primary exit: Stairwell A. Secondary exit: Stairwell B. Assembly point: parking lot P2.

On every floor, the elevators stop running automatically the moment the alarm sounds. Never use them to leave the building. Remain at your assembly point until a warden gives the all-clear signal.
