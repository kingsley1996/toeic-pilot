NOTICE TO ALL TENANTS

Date: June 10
From: Building Management Office, Harborview Office Center
To: All tenant companies

Please be advised that the building's elevators will be out of service this coming weekend for scheduled maintenance. To allow technicians to upgrade the elevator control systems, electrical power to the elevator shafts will be shut off from 7:00 A.M. on Saturday, June 21, until 6:00 P.M. on Sunday, June 22.

During this period, please use the stairwells located at both ends of each floor. Please note that the stairwell doors remain locked from the outside for security reasons, so tenants who exit through a stairwell will need their access cards to re-enter the building.

Regular elevator service will resume on Monday morning, June 23. Tenants who need assistance transporting equipment during the shutdown, or who have questions about the work, should contact the management office at extension 205 or visit the lobby desk.

We apologize for any inconvenience and thank you for your cooperation.

— Building Management Office
