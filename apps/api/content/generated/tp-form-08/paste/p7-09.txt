Younger Customers Prefer Buying Insurance Online
By the Market Research Team

A recent market survey of 1,200 customers aged 22 to 35 found that 68 percent of respondents purchased their most recent policy entirely online, up from 41 percent two years ago. [1] Participants cited fast price comparisons, instant quotes, and the ability to finish paperwork on a phone as their main reasons for avoiding branch visits.

Visitors to the company's website frequently begin an application on the day they first view a policy. [2]

Most young buyers also read customer reviews before choosing coverage, and nearly half said they would switch providers for an easier app. [3]

To keep this group, the digital team will launch a simplified mobile sign-up next quarter and shorten the claims form from nine pages to three. [4] "Speed and transparency decide whether these customers stay with us," said product manager Lena Ortiz. Branch staff will be trained to guide online buyers rather than compete with the website.
