Review: The Study Room Café Brings Work Space Downtown

By Lena Ortiz, Staff Writer

The work-space café chain The Study Room opened its first downtown location on Maple Avenue last week, and the early response has been enthusiastic. The café offers more than forty seats, fast wireless internet, private booths for video calls, and unlimited drip coffee for guests who book a day pass online. Prices start at eight dollars for a half day, noticeably less than nearby coworking offices charge. Manager Priya Nair says the downtown site was chosen for its two-minute walk from the central train station, which makes it convenient for commuters, and the chain plans three more city locations by next spring.

Customer comments collected by this magazine have been mostly positive. "The quiet zones are a real change from other cafés," one regular wrote. Another visitor praised the staff: "They held my booth reservation even when I arrived forty minutes late." A few guests noted that the space fills up quickly after 11 A.M., so arriving early is advised.
