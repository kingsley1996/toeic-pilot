Meadowbrook Foods Distributors
412 Canal Street, Portside

Dear Ms. Ortiz,

I am writing on behalf of the Rosewood Garden Bistro regarding delivery order No. 4471, placed with your company on March 2. The order was scheduled to arrive on Friday, March 12, in time for the spring festival weekend. However, the shipment did not reach our loading dock until the afternoon of Tuesday, March 16. By then, much of the fresh produce had spoiled.

As a result, we lost an estimated $3,000 in cancelled reservations and wasted ingredients. [1] Clause 7 of our supply contract provides for compensation in such cases. [2] Specifically, late deliveries carry a penalty of 5 percent of the invoice value for each day of delay. [3] By our calculation, the penalty on this order, valued at $12,000, comes to $2,400. [4]

I would like to note that this is the first delivery problem we have experienced in our three years of working together, and we hope it can be resolved quickly. Please send your written reply by March 25.

Sincerely,

Marcus Bell
Director, Rosewood Garden Bistro
