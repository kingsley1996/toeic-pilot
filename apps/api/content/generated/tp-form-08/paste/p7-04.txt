Crestway Car Rentals
Drive More, Pay Less: Long-Term Rentals for Business Clients

Crestway Car Rentals is pleased to announce special discounted monthly rates for corporate clients who rent vehicles for extended periods. Businesses that rent three or more vehicles for at least ninety days will receive 25 percent off the standard monthly rate. Every vehicle in the program comes with free scheduled maintenance, full insurance coverage, and unlimited mileage. Clients may also exchange a vehicle at any time at no extra charge if their needs change.

To enroll, a company must hold a registered business account with Crestway. Accounts can be created online in less than ten minutes, and approval usually takes one business day. The program is available at all of our airport and downtown branches and runs through September 30.

For details about fleet pricing, or to request a written quotation, call our corporate services desk at 555-0142 or send an e-mail to business@crestway-rentals.com. Enroll today at www.crestway-rentals.com/business.
