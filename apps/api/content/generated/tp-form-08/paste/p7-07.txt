Technology Review — Association of Independent Retailers
WareSight 4.0: Powerful, but Not Cheap
By Daniel Okafor, technology columnist

Last month, the developers of WareSight released version 4.0 of their warehouse management software. I spent three weeks testing the program at a mid-sized distribution center run by a member of this association. The upgrade's most impressive feature is its demand-forecasting tool, which predicted weekly stock needs with remarkable accuracy after just ten days of use.

Installation took only five days, well under the industry norm of two weeks. Smaller warehouses, however, may find the pricing steep: a license costs $1,200 per terminal, while rival packages such as TrackLine and ShelfMate charge under $900. Where WareSight clearly wins is customer support; during my trial, every call to its help desk was answered in under three minutes.

On the whole, I recommend WareSight 4.0 for larger operations that can absorb the cost. Smaller businesses should wait for the mid-tier edition scheduled for next spring, which the company says will offer most of the forecasting features at a lower price.
