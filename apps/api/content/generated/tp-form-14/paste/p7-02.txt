MediBook: Doctor Appointments Without the Phone Queue

The MediBook app lets patients reserve slots at nearly five hundred clinics across the country. Instead of calling during office hours, office workers can book during their lunch break in under a minute.

The app also sends reminders the day before each visit, so missed appointments have dropped sharply since its rollout last year. Downloading MediBook is free, though some clinics charge a small booking fee. Video visits with specialists begin next spring. Prescriptions can be renewed inside the app without extra fees. A family profile covers up to five members.

(B) Nearly five hundred

Answer: B
