Subject: Proposal to Reprint "The River Market"
To: An Tran, Author
From: Mekong Books

Dear Ms. Tran,

Q4 sales hit a record, and our warehouse stock runs low. We propose a reprint of ten thousand copies with a new cover. Translation rights sold in three countries last spring. The audiobook topped charts for six weeks. Royalty statements arrive each quarter by mail. The new edition would keep the original illustrations and the same price on the back cover, printed this time on lighter uncoated paper. Reader letters asking for a sequel reach the office every week, and two school reading programmes have added the book to their summer lists.

Please review the attached contract. We would love to keep working with you.

[PASSAGE]
Dear Ms. Tran,

We are pleased to invite you to the signing ceremony for the reprint contract at our Hanoi office on July 5. The reprint adds two new chapters about riverside life. A launch party follows the ceremony downtown. Journalists may request interviews afterwards. The cover photo shoot takes place next Monday. Wine and snacks follow the signing. Ms. Tran will say a few words about the book's origins before guests sign copies at the long table. Sixty seats in the reading room are held for readers, and the ceremony is open to the public from four o'clock. Photographers from two newspapers will attend, so the front room stays closed to visitors for the first half hour.

Please send a portrait photo for the cover before June 25.

Best regards,
Mekong Books
