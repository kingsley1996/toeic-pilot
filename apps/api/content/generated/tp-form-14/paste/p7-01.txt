Weekend Banh Mi Baking Class — Flour & Fire Bakery

Learn to bake crusty banh mi in a single Saturday morning. Our instructor Lan has baked bread for twelve years and guides every step from mixing to scoring. No experience is needed, and all ingredients are included in the fee.

Classes run every Saturday from eight to eleven, with a maximum of ten learners. The fee is 450,000 dong per person. Please register by Thursday evening, as weekend classes often fill up early.

The bakery is located at 45 Tran Phu Street, a five-minute walk from the central market. Parking for motorbikes is available in front of the shop. All tools and aprons are provided at the door. Students take home the loaves they bake, still warm from the oven. Private group bookings open every December.
