Notice: Scheduled Water Cut on Sunday

The management board informs residents that water supply will be cut on Sunday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. for pipeline maintenance. Please store enough water for cooking and bathing during that time.

The pumps on the rooftop will also be cleaned, so water may run weak for one hour after supply returns. For urgent needs, contact the management office at extension 102. The rooftop pumps serve floors ten to fifteen only. Residents above floor fifteen use a separate tank on the north side. Bottled water stations open in the lobby during the cut. Elevator service continues normally throughout Sunday. Report leaks to extension 102 immediately.

[QUESTION]

(A) A pipeline repair meeting
(B) A scheduled water cut
(C) A management fee increase

Answer: B
Explanation: Ban quản lý thông báo nguồn nước sẽ bị cắt vào Chủ nhật từ 8 giờ sáng đến 5 giờ chiều để bảo trì đường ống. | (A) "A pipeline repair meeting" — bài không đề cập đến bất kỳ cuộc họp nào. | (B) "A scheduled water cut" — đúng nội dung chính của thông báo. | (C) "A management fee increase" — không có thông tin về việc tăng phí quản lý. | (D) "A power outage plan" — thông báo nói về nước, không phải điện.
