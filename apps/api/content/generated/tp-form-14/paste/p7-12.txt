Notice: New Night Market Hours from July

The management board announces new opening hours for the night market. From July 1, the market opens at 5 p.m. and closes at 11 p.m. daily.

All vendors must park in the east lot behind the food stalls. The main gate closes thirty minutes after closing time. The stall fee stays the same. Stall maps hang at both entrances from Monday. Lost items go to the office behind gate two. Fire lanes must stay clear after dark. Music stops at ten thirty sharp. Security patrols the aisles until midnight. New vendors apply at the east office.

[PASSAGE]
To: Night Market Board
From: Thanh, Food Stall 12

Dear Board,

I read the new hours notice. Is the stall fee higher this year? Also, can I keep my corner spot near the main gate? My cousin shares the next stall and splits power costs. We close early on rainy evenings. Thank you for reading my long message.
