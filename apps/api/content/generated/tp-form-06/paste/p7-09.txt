Westside Career Expo to Return Next Month

The annual Westside Career Expo will take place on November 14 at the Grandview Convention Center. [1] This year, organizers expect over 80 local businesses to participate, representing sectors such as healthcare, technology, and hospitality. [2] Attendees will have the opportunity to meet directly with hiring managers, submit resumes, and even participate in on-the-spot interviews. Additionally, several free workshops on resume writing and interview techniques will be offered throughout the day. [3] Registration is free for all job seekers, but pre-registration online is highly recommended to avoid long lines at the entrance. [4] Parking at the convention center will also be discounted for all registered attendees.
