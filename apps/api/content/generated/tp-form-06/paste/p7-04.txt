Careers at Apex Logistics Solutions

Position: Warehouse Coordinator

Apex Logistics Solutions is seeking an experienced Warehouse Coordinator to oversee daily operations at our distribution center in Westside Industrial Park. 

Key Responsibilities:
- Coordinate incoming and outgoing shipments to ensure timely delivery.
- Monitor inventory levels and order supplies when necessary.
- Supervise a team of ten warehouse associates.
- Utilize database systems to record stock movements.

Requirements:
- A minimum of three years of experience in logistics or warehouse management.
- Proficiency in inventory database systems.
- Ability to lift packages up to 50 pounds.
- Flexibility to work rotating weekend shifts.

We offer a competitive hourly wage, comprehensive health benefits, and opportunities for professional growth. Interested candidates should submit a resume and a brief cover letter to hiring@apexlogistics.com by October 25. Please note that only selected candidates will be contacted for interviews, which are scheduled to begin in early November.
