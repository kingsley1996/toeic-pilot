NOTICE

To: All Authorized Distributors
From: Apex Office Solutions, Product Management Division
Date: June 10
Subject: Launch of Ergo X500 Task Chair

Apex Office Solutions is pleased to announce the upcoming release of the Ergo X500, our most advanced ergonomic task chair to date. The official launch date is July 1. Distributors may begin accepting pre-orders on June 15 through the Apex Partner Portal.

As a valued partner, you are eligible for a 15% distributor discount on all Ergo X500 units ordered before August 31. Standard volume tiers apply for orders exceeding 100 units. Promotional marketing kits, including product specification sheets and lifestyle imagery, will be available for download from the Partner Portal starting June 12.

For questions regarding the discount structure or marketing assets, please contact Ms. Elena Vance, Partner Relations Manager, at evance@apex-officesolutions.com.
