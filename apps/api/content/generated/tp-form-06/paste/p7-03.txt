Notice: Temporary Closure of West Parking Lot

Please be advised that the West Parking Lot will be closed for resurfacing and line-painting starting on Monday, October 12, at 6:00 A.M. The project is expected to take approximately four days, with the lot scheduled to fully reopen on Friday, October 16.

During this period, all permit holders for the West Parking Lot may park in the East Garage at no additional charge. To gain access, please scan your regular parking permit card at the East Garage entrance gate.

Please note that vehicles remaining in the West Parking Lot after 11:00 P.M. on Sunday, October 11, will be towed at the owner's expense to ensure the maintenance crew can begin work on schedule.

We apologize for any inconvenience this temporary disruption may cause and appreciate your cooperation in improving our facility. For questions or assistance, contact the Facilities Management Office at extension 4402.
