**NOTICE TO ALL TENANTS**
Meridian Office Complex – Building Management Office
Date: March 10

As part of our annual safety program, scheduled maintenance will be performed on the passenger elevators in Building C. The service work will take place on Saturday, March 22, from 7:00 A.M. to approximately 4:00 P.M.

During this time, both passenger elevators will be out of operation. Tenants and visitors may use the freight elevator located at the east corridor for building access. Stairwells will also remain available on all floors.

We kindly ask that any large deliveries or equipment moves be rescheduled to avoid congestion at the freight elevator.

If you have questions or concerns, please contact the Building Management Office at extension 205 or visit us in Suite 1A.

Thank you for your cooperation.
—Meridian Office Complex Management

[QUESTION]
What is the main purpose of this notice?
(A) To announce a change in building ownership
(B) To inform tenants about scheduled elevator maintenance
(C) To introduce new freight elevator procedures
(D) To remind tenants of monthly fire drill requirements
Answer: B
Explanation: Thông báo mở đầu bằng "scheduled maintenance will be performed on the passenger elevators in Building C", xác định mục đích chính là thông báo cho người thuê về đợt bảo trì sắp tới. | (A) "To announce a change in building ownership" — không có thông tin nào về chuyển nhượng tòa nhà. | (B) "To inform tenants about scheduled elevator maintenance" — đúng nội dung mở đầu vừa dẫn. | (C) "To introduce new freight elevator procedures" — thang chở hàng chỉ được nhắc đến như phương án tạm thời, không phải quy trình mới. | (D) "To remind tenants of monthly fire drill requirements" — không đề cập diễn tập chữa cháy.
