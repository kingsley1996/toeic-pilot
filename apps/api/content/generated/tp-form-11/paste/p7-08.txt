**Local Dining Review: Verdant Table**
By Alicia Gomez

Verdant Table, a contemporary plant-based bistro, recently opened its doors on Elmwood Avenue. As a resident of the area, I was eager to determine whether this new dining spot could successfully expand our neighborhood's culinary variety. To celebrate its launch, the restaurant is currently offering a complimentary dessert to every guest who orders an entrée before 6:00 P.M.

During my visit on Thursday evening, I sampled the roasted squash risotto. The dish demonstrated remarkable creativity, combining rich herbs with fresh, locally sourced vegetables. Diners will appreciate that the chef updates the dinner selections every two weeks to reflect the changing agricultural harvest. 

The service was efficient throughout my meal, with the waitstaff readily answering questions about gluten-free preparation. Although the dining room has modest dimensions with only ten tables, the cozy setting provides an inviting atmosphere. Keep in mind that the introductory dessert special applies exclusively to weekday visits and ends after this month. Overall, Verdant Table delivers outstanding food, and I encourage local food enthusiasts to frequent this delightful new establishment.
