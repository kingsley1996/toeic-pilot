**Metroville Business Review**
October 14

Solstice Coffee, a rapidly expanding regional beverage company known for ethical sourcing, officially opened its first downtown Metroville location yesterday on Elm Street. The firm has already recruited 25 full-time staff members from the surrounding municipal area to manage daily cafe operations.

To distinguish itself from established competitors in the district, Solstice Coffee transformed its second floor into a quiet co-working lounge equipped with individual workstations and high-speed Internet access. Furthermore, patrons who bring a reusable container during the store's inaugural week will receive a complimentary pastry with every specialty beverage purchase.

According to Chief Executive Officer Nathan Drake, the brand invested substantially in energy-efficient espresso machines to minimize electricity usage. Mr. Drake noted that if customer attendance remains robust throughout the initial quarter, management intends to launch two suburban drive-through outlets before the end of the current fiscal year.

What amenity is available on the second floor of Solstice Coffee?
(A) An outdoor patio
(B) A quiet workspace
(C) A baking kitchen
(D) A conference room
Answer: B
