From: registration@futureworksummit.example
To: all registrants
Subject: Before you arrive
Date: October 2

Dear Participant,

Thank you for registering for the Future Work Technology Summit. Please bring a printed or digital copy of your registration form. Badge pickup opens at 7:30 A.M. in the North Lobby and closes at 9:00 A.M. If you miss this window, collect your badge at the Help Desk in Exhibit Hall 2.

Session rooms are assigned by registration category. Standard registrants attend the morning keynote in Room A. Premium registrants attend the same keynote in Room B, which includes a reserved seat and a printed workbook. Lunch choices are linked to your session: attendees in Room A receive boxed meals, while attendees in Room B receive hot meals.

If your form lists a dietary option, present it at badge pickup. Late arrivals who collect badges at the Help Desk should show the same form before entering the exhibit hall.

Exhibit Hall 2 Layout Notice
Updated October 3

The exhibit hall opens at 8:00 A.M. and closes at 5:00 P.M. The Help Desk is located between Booth 14 and the east entrance. Booth 14 is the registration information booth, and staff there can reprint lost badges. Room A is directly across from the coffee counter. Room B is next to the networking lounge and behind Booth 22. Booth 22 is the sponsor booth for the afternoon demonstrations.

Meal pickup tables are placed near the rooms they serve. The boxed meal pickup table is beside Room A. The hot meal pickup table is beside the networking lounge. The vegetarian meal pickup table is beside the Help Desk. Attendees should not take meals into the session rooms. A quiet room is available near the west entrance for participants who need a break. The sponsor booth closes at 4:30 P.M. Please keep aisles clear.
