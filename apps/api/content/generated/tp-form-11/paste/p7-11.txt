**Shieldstone Insurance — Corporate Notice**
**Subject:** Grand Opening Celebration: New Headquarters
**Date:** October 15

We are pleased to announce the official opening of Shieldstone Insurance’s new global headquarters at Horizon Plaza on Friday, October 24. This state-of-the-art facility brings our regional operations, claims processing, and underwriting teams under one roof for the first time.

**Event Itinerary:**
- **9:30 A.M.** – Registration and welcome refreshments (Main Atrium)
- **10:30 A.M.** – Ribbon-cutting ceremony & address by the CEO (Grand Auditorium, Level 2)
- **12:00 P.M.** – Buffet luncheon (Skyline Lounge, Level 15)
- **1:45 P.M.** – Guided tours of departmental workspaces, starting with the teams that arrived on the first morning shuttle
- **3:00 P.M.** – Event concludes

Please note that business formal attire is required. Because underground parking at Horizon Plaza is reserved strictly for senior executives and client delegates, personal vehicles will not be permitted in the facility garage. All other personnel must take the chartered shuttle buses departing from our West End branch.

**MEMORANDUM**
**To:** All Personnel
**From:** Logistics & Facilities Committee
**Date:** October 17
**Subject:** Transportation Schedule for October 24 Event

To ensure everyone arrives smoothly for the opening event at Horizon Plaza, chartered coaches will run from the West End branch parking lot (420 Elm Street). Please check your designated departure time below:

- **Shuttle 1 (Departs 8:40 A.M.):** Claims Processing and Customer Relations
- **Shuttle 2 (Departs 8:55 A.M.):** Actuarial Analysis and Underwriting
- **Shuttle 3 (Departs 9:10 A.M.):** Information Technology, Legal, and Human Resources

All coaches take approximately 30 minutes to reach Horizon Plaza. Return services will depart from the plaza entrance at 3:15 P.M., 3:45 P.M., and 4:15 P.M.

Employees needing accessibility accommodations should notify Martin Vance in Fleet Management no later than October 20 so modified transport can be arranged. Anyone who fails to board their assigned shuttle will be responsible for their own transit and must check in at Security Gate B upon arrival.
