**SILVERDALE DAILY HERALD**
**Council Approves Major Expansion for Silverdale Regional Airport**
*By David Miller*
*November 14*

The Silverdale City Council voted unanimously on Tuesday to approve the long-debated Phase II expansion plan for Silverdale Regional Airport. [1] The $45 million initiative is designed to handle rising passenger volumes and attract direct flights from major international carriers.

Under the approved plan, the facility will construct a new passenger concourse featuring six additional departure gates, dedicated customs facilities, and modernized baggage handling systems. [2] In addition, Runway 3 will be extended by 800 meters to accommodate larger wide-body aircraft. Construction is scheduled to commence in March and will conclude before the busy holiday travel season in late 2027. [3] Funding for the project will come from federal transportation grants, with the remainder covered by municipal airport revenue bonds.

To address persistent noise concerns from nearby neighborhoods, airport authorities agreed to several concessions. A sound insulation subsidy will be offered to property owners located within two miles of the southern runway corridor. Furthermore, the mandatory curfew prohibiting departures and landings between 11:00 P.M. and 5:30 A.M. will remain strictly in effect. A public forum will be held at the Silverdale Community Center next Thursday evening to allow residents to review detailed architectural blueprints. [4]
