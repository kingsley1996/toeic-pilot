A wide glass-walled airport corridor with polished floors. Four passengers walk in a loose line, each pulling a wheeled suitcase: a woman at the front in a dark coat looking straight ahead; a man behind her glancing down at a smartphone in his free hand; two more passengers at the back talking to each other. Empty plastic chairs line the left wall, and a departure information board is mounted above them. Outside the large glass windows on the right, the tarmac is visible with yellow ground markings. Two ground crew workers in orange high-visibility vests are pushing a flat baggage cart loaded with suitcases across the tarmac. The cart is several meters from a belt loader positioned at the open cargo hold of a white commercial jet aircraft. In the far background another aircraft is parked and a small utility vehicle is moving along the tarmac.
