Business Logistics Monthly
October 4

In a recent market review, Apex Express was highlighted for its newly introduced corporate shipping program designed to support growing enterprises. The express delivery provider now offers tailored service tiers that allow local businesses to streamline their dispatch operations. Under the standard program, commercial clients receive a baseline 10 percent discount on all domestic packages. However, as part of an autumn promotional campaign, companies that sign a formal agreement before October 15 will be granted a 20 percent discount on all shipping rates for their first six months. Additionally, corporate accounts gain access to complimentary packaging supplies and a dedicated customer service representative. Early partner feedback indicates high satisfaction with the service's speed and reliability, making it a competitive choice for small businesses seeking cost-effective logistics solutions.
