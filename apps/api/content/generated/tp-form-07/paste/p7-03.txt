NOTICE

TO: All Office Employees
FROM: Human Resources Department
DATE: October 18
SUBJECT: Revised Operating Hours

Please be advised that starting November 1, the company will implement new standard working hours for all office personnel. This adjustment is intended to better align our daily operations with our international partners and clients across different time zones.

Under the new schedule, regular full-time working hours will shift from 8:30 A.M.–5:30 P.M. to 9:00 A.M.–6:00 P.M., Monday through Friday. Please note that while part-time staff members will maintain their currently contracted number of total hours, their shift start times will move 30 minutes later to coincide with the updated office schedule. 

All employees are requested to update their work calendars accordingly. If you have scheduling conflicts or questions regarding how this change impacts your routine, please contact your department supervisor or the HR office by October 25.

Thank you for your cooperation during this transition.
