Notice: Operating Guidelines for Model X-90 Photocopier
To: All Staff & Technical Personnel
From: IT & Facilities Management
Date: October 12

The new Model X-90 multipurpose photocopier is now fully operational in the main copy room. Please review the following operating guidelines and troubleshooting steps:

- Color Printing: By default, the unit operates in black-and-white mode to conserve color toner. To print or copy in color, users must enter their 4-digit department access code on the machine's touch screen interface before starting the job.
- Frequent Paper Jams: If you experience recurring paper jams, the first step is to adjust the paper width guides inside the tray to ensure they fit snugly against the paper stack without bending it. After adjusting the guides, verify that paper is not loaded above the maximum fill line.
- Technical Support: For issues requiring internal component maintenance, please contact techsupport@example.com.
