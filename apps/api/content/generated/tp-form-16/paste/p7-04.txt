Lan Pham [2:10 P.M.]
Hi Hung. Can you pick up Minh from school today? I have a dentist appointment at three that I cannot move.

Hung Do [2:15 P.M.]
Yes, but my car is in the garage until Friday. I'll take the bus and be at the school by three.

Lan Pham [2:20 P.M.]
School ends at half past three. Wait for him at the main gate with the other parents.

Hung Do [2:25 P.M.]
OK. I'll bring his football boots too. Training starts at four.

Lan Pham [2:30 P.M.]
His red schoolbag is by the front door. Please grab it.

Hung Do [2:35 P.M.]
Got it. Does training end at five as usual? Traffic looks heavy and I might be five minutes late.

Lan Pham [2:40 P.M.]
Yes. Bring him straight home after training, dinner is at six.

Lan Pham [2:50 P.M.]
Thanks! Minh's allergy medicine is in the bag's front pocket.

[QUESTION]
Why can't Lan pick up Minh?
(A) She forgot the time
(B) Her car broke down
(C) She is stuck at work
(D) She has a dentist appointment
Answer: D
Explanation: Lan nhờ vì "I have a dentist appointment at three that I cannot move". | (A) "She forgot the time" — cô nhớ rõ giờ hẹn nha sĩ. | (B) "Her car broke down" — xe hỏng là của Hùng, không phải Lan. | (C) "She is stuck at work" — không công việc nào được nhắc tới. | (D) "She has a dentist appointment" — đúng lý do Lan không đón Minh được.
