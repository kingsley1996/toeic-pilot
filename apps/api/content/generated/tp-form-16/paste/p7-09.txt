BlueWave Pool — New Course

Adult Beginners Swim Class

Never learned to swim? Our Monday, Wednesday and Friday classes at 6:30 A.M. take only first-timers. Coach Elena Pop, a former national team swimmer, leads every session in our heated 25-metre pool. Each class takes at most eight learners, so every swimmer gets real attention.

The pool water stays at twenty-eight degrees all year. Towels are free, but bring your own lock for lockers. The four-week course costs $60 including cap and goggles. Private lessons cost twice the group price. The first lesson is free, but registration closes this Friday and late entries cannot join halfway.

[QUESTION]
What can be inferred about class places?
(A) Late entries pay double
(B) Hundreds can join each class
(C) Registration never closes
(D) They are limited and fill quickly
Answer: D
Explanation: Mỗi lớp "at most eight learners" và "registration closes this Friday". | (A) "Late entries pay double" — học viên muộn không được vào chứ không trả gấp đôi. | (B) "Hundreds can join each class" — ngược hẳn: tối đa tám người. | (C) "Registration never closes" — đăng ký đóng thứ Sáu này. | (D) "They are limited and fill quickly" — đúng điều suy ra về số chỗ.
