From: Riverside Fair Committee
To: All Stall Owners
Subject: Saturday Food Fair — Final Details
Date: October 2

Dear Owners,

The autumn food fair opens this Saturday at nine in the morning, rain or shine. The cooking contest starts at noon on the central stage, and judges will visit every stall before eleven. Expect bustling crowds from nearby schools, so keep your counters stocked all day.

Our crepe stall is right across from the juice stand, so follow the smell of fresh juice to find us. Enter through the north gate where volunteers hand out maps. Parking is behind the main hall. The contest winner receives five hundred dollars and a trophy. Stall owners must clean their zone before eight in the evening. Free drinking water stations stand beside every toilet block. Last year over three thousand visitors came through the gates. Dogs are welcome but must stay on a leash. Winners are announced at five on the central stage. Stall fees include power and two chairs. Lost children are brought to the information tent. The fair ends at nine with fireworks. Bus shuttles run every twenty minutes from the station. Food safety inspectors visit every stall at ten. The committee provides free ice to all stalls. Noise must stop by eight in the evening.

See you Saturday!
