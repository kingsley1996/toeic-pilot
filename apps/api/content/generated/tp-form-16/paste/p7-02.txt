City Eats Weekly — App Review

FastBite Promises Dinner in Thirty Minutes

By Tom Alvarez

The new delivery app FastBite guarantees an average delivery time of thirty minutes across the downtown area, and our three test orders arrived in twenty-four, twenty-nine, and thirty-one minutes. Unlike rivals stuffed with fast-food chains, FastBite lists a curated set of eighty local kitchens chosen by neighbourhood chefs. Prices match the restaurants' own menus, and the delivery fee is a flat one dollar fifty per order.

Riders wear green jackets and carry insulated boxes. The app works on old phones without updates. These kitchens pass yearly hygiene checks. Suburban readers can join a waiting list. A loyalty point comes with every tenth order. Payment is painless, and live tracking actually works. Our only complaint: the service area still excludes the suburbs, so readers outside downtown must wait for expansion.
