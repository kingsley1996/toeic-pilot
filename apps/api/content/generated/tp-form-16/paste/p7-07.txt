City Hall Bulletin Board

Blood Donation Day — Give an Hour, Save a Life

The Red Cross holds its spring blood drive this Saturday from 8 to 11 A.M. in the City Hall main lobby. All donors receive a free health check, and nurses record blood pressure and iron levels before every donation. First-time donors are welcome; bring an ID card, be at least eighteen, and eat breakfast beforehand.

Each donor also gets a gift voucher and a donor card for priority booking next time. The drive supports the children's hospital downtown. Parking is free for donors behind City Hall. Bring a friend and both get extra gift tickets. Last spring the city collected 400 units. Let's beat that record!
