Head Office Notice: Tet Holiday Schedule

Dear Staff,

The office closes from January 28 to January 31 for the Tet holiday. No regular services run during these four days. An emergency duty team stays each day to receive urgent deliveries and handle incidents. For urgent issues, call Mr. Long at 090-444-1188 at any hour.

January payslips are available online from the twenty-fifth. The parking garage closes at ten each night of the holiday. Party decorations must come down by February 2. Security patrols double around the warehouse during Tet. The canteen serves free sticky rice on January 27. Gifts for staff arrive on January 26. The elevator maintenance finishes before Tet. Firecrackers are banned inside the building. Banks close from January 29 to February 1. Translators are available for foreign staff on January 28. The New Year party starts at four on January 27. First-aid boxes are checked on January 26. Normal work resumes on February 1. Happy New Year!
