Leave Policy (summary)

All leave requests need at least seven days' notice. Leave in March requires the manager's signature as well. Requests clashing with scheduled training move to the next free week. Shift swaps need a written request. Managers answer requests within two working days. Public holidays do not count as leave days. Lunch breaks stay unchanged during training weeks. Night shifts count as one and a half days. Paternity leave is ten working days. The HR portal logs every request automatically. Wedding leave needs an invitation copy. Overtime during March is paid at double rate. Unused leave days expire at the end of June.

[QUESTION]
Why might Son's request be rejected?
(A) Ben cannot cover shifts
(B) Weddings are not valid reasons
(C) It was sent only two days ahead
(D) March leave is forbidden
Answer: C
Explanation: Đơn ghi "Date: March 1" cho kỳ nghỉ "from March 3 to March 5" còn quy định ghi "All leave requests need at least seven days' notice". | (A) "Ben cannot cover shifts" — Ben đã nhận trực thay, không ai từ chối. | (B) "Weddings are not valid reasons" — quy định không cấm lý do cưới hỏi. | (C) "sent only two days ahead" — từ mùng một tới mùng ba chưa đủ bảy ngày. | (D) "March leave is forbidden" — nghỉ tháng Ba chỉ cần thêm chữ ký, không bị cấm.
