holds up her smartphone toward the painting, and the second woman steps slightly
aside to look at it. To the right of the hall, a stone statue of a seated figure
stands on a pedestal inside a low rope barrier. Beside the pedestal, a female
guide in a dark blazer holds a folder in one hand and gestures toward the statue
with the other while speaking to two elderly visitors, a man and a woman, who
stand facing her with their hands at their sides; nobody in this second group
holds a camera or a phone. Along the far back wall, an empty wooden bench sits
beneath a tall arched doorway. Exactly six people are in the photograph, and all
of them are standing.
