Review: A Promising Start at the City-Center Coffee Chain
By staff reporter
April 3

The new city-center coffee chain opened last weekend with polished oak counters, soft lighting, and a menu that favors simple classics over trendy syrups. At launch, a small espresso costs $2.80, a drip coffee $3.10, and a flat white $3.90. Those prices are modest for a central location, and the first-floor seating feels calm enough for laptop work. Most tables also include power outlets, though only two sections have quiet zones.

Service, however, was slower than the signage suggested. A chalkboard promised drinks in under four minutes, yet my order required nine minutes on a Tuesday morning. Staff explained that the espresso machine had been misconfigured during training. That problem may disappear as the team settles, but customers should still plan for a short wait during rush hours.

The chain's loyalty app is the strongest part of the offer. Members receive 15 percent off any drink purchased before 10 A.M., and on Fridays they may add a second drink for $1.50 after the first discount is applied. To get the Friday bonus, the order must be placed through the app, not at the counter. [1] The app also sends a weekly coupon for a free pastry with a drink. [2] The pastries are baked offsite, so they arrive earlier than expected but lose warmth by afternoon. [3] If management improves speed, this chain could become a serious neighborhood favorite. [4]
