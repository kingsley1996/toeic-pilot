[Mr. Long] [9:02 A.M.]
We need the laser printer on floor three repaired before Friday. Last week it stopped feeding paper during the accounting audit, so I need it working by Thursday at 3 P.M. Can you come in this morning, preferably before noon?

[Ms. Hoa] [9:07 A.M.]
I can start at 10:15 A.M., but I will need the access code for the copy room. Maintenance left it on a sticky note last time, and I have not found a new one. Please confirm if it changed.

[Mr. Long] [9:10 A.M.]
The current code is posted inside the staff lounge, not on the door. Do not open the printer before checking the service contract. If the fault is outside the warranty, I must get approval before parts are replaced.

[Ms. Hoa] [9:16 A.M.]
Understood. I will bring the standard toolkit and a spare roller. If the roller solves the paper jam, I can finish quickly and send you a closing note. If it is the main motor, I will call you before touching anything else.

[Mr. Long] [9:22 A.M.]
Good. The floor-three manager is on a client visit until 11 A.M., so please do not use the hallway lift for the large machine. Take the freight lift.

[Ms. Hoa] [9:27 A.M.]
No problem. I can try to complete the work before lunch, but I cannot promise the printer will be ready for Thursday if the motor needs an external part.

[Mr. Long] [9:31 A.M.]
That is acceptable. If an external part is needed, tell me by noon so I can arrange delivery and notify the audit team of the new schedule.

[QUESTION]
Why are Mr. Long and Ms. Hoa exchanging these messages?
(A) To request a new copy room
(B) To report an audit result
(C) To organize a repair
(D) To cancel a service contract
Answer: C
Explanation: Tin nhắn đầu tiên nêu "We need the laser printer on floor three repaired before Friday" and asks "Can you come in this morning, preferably before noon?" | (A) "To request a new copy room" — "copy room" chỉ là nơi đặt máy cần mã vào, không phải yêu cầu phòng mới. | (B) "To report an audit result" — có nhắc "accounting audit" nhưng chỉ là lý do lịch sử, không phải báo cáo kết quả kiểm toán. | (C) "To organize a repair" — đúng vì nội dung chính là sắp xếp việc sửa máy in. | (D) "To cancel a service contract" — "service contract" cần được kiểm tra, không phải hủy.
