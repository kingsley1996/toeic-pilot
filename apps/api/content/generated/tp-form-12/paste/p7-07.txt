PREMIUM CARS — CORPORATE RENTAL PROGRAM

Need reliable transportation for employees traveling between offices, airports, and client sites? Premium Cars now offers a Corporate Rental Program designed for businesses with frequent travel needs.

Program benefits:
• A dedicated account manager to arrange vehicle pick-up and return.
• Access to a fleet of sedans, vans, and SUVs with basic insurance included.
• A 20% discount on weekly rental rates for any qualifying business.
• One free airport transfer per month for accounts that maintain at least three active rentals.

Qualifying rules:
• Companies must submit a signed corporate agreement before June 30.
• The 20% discount applies only to rentals of seven days or longer.
• Accounts must reserve at least two vehicles at the same time to receive the free airport transfer.

Employees must be at least twenty-five years old and present a valid corporate identification card when collecting a vehicle. The free airport transfer cannot be exchanged for cash or additional rental days. Call early to confirm availability.

To join, visit premiumcars.example or call your existing account representative. Space is limited, and rates may change after July 15.

What must a company do to receive the free airport transfer?
(A) Use a sedan or van and call the account representative early
(B) Sign the agreement before June 30 and rent for a week
(C) Pay a monthly fee and present identification when collecting
(D) Maintain three active rentals and reserve two vehicles simultaneously
Answer: D
Explanation: Quảng cáo nêu "One free airport transfer per month for accounts that maintain at least three active rentals" and later "Accounts must reserve at least two vehicles at the same time to receive the free airport transfer". | (A) "Use a sedan or van and call the account representative early" — có nhắc sedan/van và "Call early", nhưng không phải yêu cầu để nhận dịch vụ đưa đón sân bay. | (B) "Sign the agreement before June 30 and rent for a week" — là điều kiện ký thỏa thuận và thời hạn thuê để giảm giá, không phải điều kiện nhận airport transfer. | (C) "Pay a monthly fee and present identification when collecting" — tài liệu không nói phí hàng tháng, chỉ yêu cầu thẻ nhận dạng khi nhận xe. | (D) "Maintain three active rentals and reserve two vehicles simultaneously" — khớp với cả hai câu vừa dẫn.
