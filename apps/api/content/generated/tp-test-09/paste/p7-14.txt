Subject: Elevator maintenance
Date: Tuesday, May 6

Dear Tenants, The central elevator will be serviced one floor at a time this week. Technicians will inspect cables, door sensors, and control panels. The maintenance window is 8 a.m. to noon each day. The map shows the interruption length for each floor. Please do not use the elevator or service elevator during your floor's closure. A four-hour interruption means the floor is closed for the entire window. Warning signs will be posted during each closure.

For safety, the floor with the longest interruption will be reserved for a final load test on Thursday. All other floors will return to normal service after their inspections.

If your store needs an urgent delivery, contact the facilities office before 5 p.m. Wednesday. Requests received later will be handled during the next available closure. We appreciate your patience.

Sincerely,
Facilities Office
