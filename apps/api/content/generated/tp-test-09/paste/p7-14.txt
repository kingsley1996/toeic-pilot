NOTICE: Elevator Maintenance at Northbridge Mall
Date: June 12

Northbridge Mall will perform scheduled elevator maintenance on Saturday, June 15. Each elevator will experience a brief halt while technicians inspect cables, doors, and control panels. The maintenance map shows the expected interruption for each floor. For the floor with the longest interruption, the temporary information desk will be placed on the floor with the next lower number.

Retail tenants on any floor with an interruption of four hours or more must move displays away from elevator doors before 8 A.M. Customers should use the north stairwell or the remaining service elevator. Management apologises for the inconvenience and thanks everyone for following the posted safety notes. Technicians will begin at 7 A.M. and finish by 5 P.M. A floor is considered unavailable when its elevator doors cannot open for loading or passenger use. If an emergency occurs, press the call button and wait for a guard. Please read the map carefully before entering.

Subject: Reminder about elevator maintenance
Date: June 13

Dear store managers,

This message reminds you of the elevator maintenance notice posted yesterday. Please confirm that your staff have read the safety notes and know which stairwell to use. If your store is on a floor with a four-hour interruption, photograph the display area before 8 A.M. and send the image to tenant services. The maintenance map will be updated if a technician finishes early. Store managers should not ask customers to use freight elevators, because those cars are reserved for emergency supplies. A printed copy of the notice is available at the tenant services office. We appreciate your cooperation in keeping the mall safe and orderly. The deadline for sending images is 9 A.M., so please assign one employee to complete this task. If you cannot photograph the area, reply to this message with your store number and reason. Tenant Services will then send a guard to check the display area.

Sincerely,
Tenant Services
