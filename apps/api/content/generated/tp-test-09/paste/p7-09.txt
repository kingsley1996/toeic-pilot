[Review: Reliable Printing for a Busy Law Office]
By Dana Whitfield, March 12

When our firm needed a printing partner for case files, business cards, and client packets, we tried three local vendors. [1] The first vendor quoted a low price but missed two deadlines, and the second sent proofs with unclear margins. [2] The third, Harbor Print Services, impressed us immediately. [3] Its representative asked about our confidentiality rules, paper preferences, and delivery windows before giving a quote. [4] The quote included itemized charges for binding and color printing, so there were no surprise fees later.

Harbor Print Services deserves praise for its document preparation service. Staff members checked every file for missing pages, corrected formatting, and produced professional packets. They also offered a secure pickup option. The online ordering system was simple, and the status updates were accurate. We could also reorder a corrected packet quickly when one client name was misspelled.

The company’s customer service was strong. A project manager called within one hour of our first request and answered questions without pressuring us to sign a long contract. Delivery was on time, and the products looked polished.

We did not use the company’s large-format banner printing, so we cannot comment on that service. The price was higher than the first vendor’s estimate, but the reliability and careful handling made the difference worthwhile. For a law office that values accuracy and confidentiality, Harbor Print Services is a sensible choice.
