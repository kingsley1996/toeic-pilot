Review: Reliable Printing for a Busy Law Office
By M. Alvarez, Office Manager
March 12

Our firm recently switched to a local print shop after our old vendor missed several deadlines. The new service has been a welcome change, especially for documents that must be finished quickly. The staff can print, trim, and bind court filings within two hours when we submit files before noon. That same-day binding option is the feature we praise most, because it saves us from rescheduling meetings with clients. We also appreciate that the staff understands confidentiality. They never leave unfinished files on the counter, and they ask us to confirm sensitive client names before printing.

The online ordering system is simple. We upload contracts, select paper weight, and choose delivery or pickup. [1] The confirmation e-mail lists the order number, the expected completion time, and the total cost. [2] For large jobs, the shop calls to check whether we want extra copies or a different cover. [3] This personal check has prevented several mistakes that would have been costly in court.

The delivery drivers are polite and arrive within the promised window. They also scan receipts and send updates if traffic delays occur. [4] The only drawback is that the shop closes early on Fridays, so late-week requests must wait until Monday. Still, the overall quality is high, and the prices are reasonable for the level of service. We recommend this print shop to any office that needs dependable, fast, and careful document preparation.
