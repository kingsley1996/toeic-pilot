Advance Bank Training presents Customer Care for Banking Professionals, a six-week online course designed to help staff handle difficult conversations, explain financial products clearly, and build trust with customers. Classes are held twice a week in the evenings, and all sessions are recorded for later review. Participants will practice real branch scenarios through live role-play, receive feedback from experienced trainers, and complete a short final assessment. Those who pass will receive a digital certificate.

Tuition is $450. Employees of partner banks receive a 20 percent discount. Groups of five or more from the same institution receive an additional $50 off per person. Early registration before June 10 saves $30. A limited number of scholarships are available for junior tellers who demonstrate strong customer-service potential.

Each participant receives an online handbook, a set of conversation templates, and access to a private discussion board where trainers answer questions within one business day. The course is also suitable for tellers, service representatives, and branch supervisors.

For details, visit www.examplebanktraining.com or call 555-0142. Seats are limited, and registration closes when the class is full.
