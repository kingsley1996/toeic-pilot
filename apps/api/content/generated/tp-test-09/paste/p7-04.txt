Customer Care for Banking Professionals

Improve service at every counter. This four-week online course teaches tellers, loan officers, and branch managers how to handle difficult customers, explain fees clearly, and protect privacy. Sessions are recorded for employees who cannot attend live classes.

Tuition is $240. Bank employees who register by June 10 receive a 25 percent discount. Branch managers who enroll three or more staff members receive an additional $20 off each registration.

Classes begin June 17 and meet Tuesdays and Thursdays from 6:00 to 7:30 P.M. Participants must have a work e-mail address and a supervisor's approval. Certificates are awarded after the final quiz.

The course includes role-play exercises, a guide to complaint records, and a live question-and-answer hour with experienced service trainers. Employees who complete all assignments may request a printed handbook at no charge. Late registrations are accepted only if seats remain, and no discount applies after June 10. Payment must be made by credit card or branch account transfer before the first class.

For more information, contact training@bankcare.example or visit the intranet page. Seats are limited to 30 per session.
