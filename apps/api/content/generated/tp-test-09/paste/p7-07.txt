Hybrid Work at Northbridge Tech

Northbridge Tech has moved from a fully remote model to a hybrid schedule, and employees are adjusting quickly. Under the new policy, staff work in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, while other days are remote. The change is intended to strengthen teamwork without eliminating the flexibility that many workers value.

To make the transition smooth, the company has asked employees to prepare in three ways. First, each person must bring a company laptop, a secure badge, and a notebook to office days. Second, workers should check the shared calendar before booking a desk, because seating is limited. Third, teams are expected to agree on core hours so that colleagues can meet face to face. Employees who travel from outside the city can request a later start time on office days.

The office has been redesigned with quiet booths, small meeting rooms, and a shared kitchen. Managers say the space feels more collaborative, though some employees still miss the calm of home. Northbridge will review the policy after six months and may adjust the required office days.
