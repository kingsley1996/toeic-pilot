NOTICE TO ALL STAFF
Building Management will carry out pest-control spraying on Level 4 on Friday, May 16, from 8 p.m. to 6 a.m. The treatment is intended to reduce insects that can damage stored paper.

To protect records from chemical residue, all paper documents that must be preserved must be placed in the sealed plastic bins provided at the reception desk before 6 p.m. on Thursday, May 15. Desks, shelves, and printers must be cleared of food, cups, and personal papers. Do not use cardboard boxes, because they can absorb moisture and chemicals.

The bins will be collected at 6 p.m. on Thursday and returned after ventilation on Monday, May 19. Employees who work from home on Friday should still follow the rule. If a bin is full, ask the floor warden for another one. The bins are available from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. at reception.

[PASSAGE]
MEMO FROM OFFICE MANAGER
To: Level 4 staff
From: Office Manager
Date: May 12
Subject: Document preservation during pest control

Please read the building notice before moving files. The sealed bins are for original client contracts, signed invoices, and personnel records only. Draft reports, printed e-mails, and meeting notes should be shredded or placed in the locked recycling cabinet.

If a file contains both a signed invoice and a draft copy, place only the signed invoice in the bin. Do not place electronic devices, paper clips, or staplers in the bins.

The blue bins are assigned to the sales and finance teams; the green bins are for human resources. The floor warden will check that each bin is labeled with the team name and the date May 15. Any bin without a label will remain in the hallway until Monday. The office manager will inspect the hallway at 6:30 p.m. and report any unsealed bins.
