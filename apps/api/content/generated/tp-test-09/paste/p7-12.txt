NOTICE TO ALL STAFF
From: Building Management
Date: 10 June
Subject: Insecticide treatment and document storage

On Thursday, 12 June, a licensed contractor will spray the third-floor work areas from 19:00 to 23:00. The product is low-odor and safe for office furniture after it dries. To protect paper records, employees must remove loose documents from desks before 18:00 and place them in the container marked Code 7. Do not leave files, notebooks, or printed reports on open surfaces. Desks that are not cleared may be photographed for a follow-up report. The treatment will not affect electronic equipment, but staff should cover keyboards with the cloths provided at the reception desk. The contractor will also treat the storage room behind the break area. Employees who keep paper copies in that room should move them to a sealed container as well. The building will remain open during the treatment, but access to the third floor will be restricted after 18:30.

[PASSAGE]
SECOND NOTICE: CODE 7 CONTAINERS
From: Facilities Office
Date: 10 June

Code 7 identifies the sealed plastic bins placed outside Room 204. These bins are available from 16:00 on 12 June and must be returned to the same location by 08:00 on Friday, 13 June. The bins are waterproof and lined with chemical-resistant material. They are intended only for paper documents during the treatment. Employees should not place food, liquids, or personal items in them. If a bin is full, use the next available bin; do not stack boxes in the corridor. A list of bin locations is posted in the elevator lobby. Staff who need a larger container may request one from the facilities desk on the second floor. The request must be made before 17:00, because deliveries stop at 17:30. Labels for Code 7 bins are printed in red and white. Any container without that label is not approved for storage during the spraying.
