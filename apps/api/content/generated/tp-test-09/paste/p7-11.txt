From: hr@example-company.com
To: all-staff@example-company.com
Subject: Annual Leave Survey Results
Date: June 12

Dear Colleagues,

We are pleased to share the results of the annual leave survey. The purpose of the survey was to understand how employees use their paid time off and to identify departments that may need additional staffing support during peak periods. The survey was voluntary, and all responses were kept confidential. Employees were asked to report the number of leave days they took during the previous twelve months and to comment on whether taking leave created difficulties for their teams. A total of 53 employees responded, representing all four departments. The response rate was higher than last year, which gives us greater confidence in the results.

The figures below were compiled from responses received between May 1 and May 31. Each department's average number of leave days per employee is shown in the chart. For planning purposes, please note the current headcounts: Sales has 8 employees, IT has 10, HR has 20, and Ops has 15. These headcounts are needed when comparing total leave days across departments, because the chart reports averages rather than totals.

The survey also asked whether employees felt comfortable taking leave without disrupting their teams. Most respondents said yes, but several noted that project deadlines can make time off difficult. In addition, the survey asked whether employees had enough notice of scheduled projects. Some said that short notice made it hard to arrange leave, especially in Sales and Ops. We will discuss these comments at the next departmental meeting and consider whether flexible scheduling could reduce pressure during busy periods.

Thank you for participating. If you have questions about the results, please contact the HR office. Please review the chart before preparing your staffing plans for the second half of the year. The HR office will also provide a summary of common comments by department.

Sincerely,
Nina Alvarez
Human Resources Director
