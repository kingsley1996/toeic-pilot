Review: Warehouse Management Software for Logistics Teams
By L. Tran

The software has helped our regional distribution center reduce misplaced pallets by nearly thirty percent since March. The dashboard shows incoming shipments, storage zones, and staff assignments in one view, which makes morning planning faster. Barcode scanning is simple, and the mobile app works well even in aisles with weak Wi-Fi. Reports on delayed orders are clear enough for supervisors to act without extra training. We tested it across two shifts and found that the alerts for low stock arrived before shortages became urgent. That alone saved time for supervisors. It also reduced overtime.

The main weakness is the setup process. Importing old inventory records took longer than expected, and several fields had to be corrected manually. The vendor’s support team answered questions within a day, but the online guide assumes users already understand warehouse codes. For a company with a small IT staff, this can slow the first month.

Still, the software is worth considering if your team needs better visibility and can spare time for initial data cleanup.
