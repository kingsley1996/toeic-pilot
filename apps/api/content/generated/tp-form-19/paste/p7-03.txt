Maple Ward People's Committee Notice: Water Suspension on October 20
Dear residents, The water supply on Maple Street and Pine Alley will be suspended from 9:00 A.M. to 5:00 P.M. on Monday, October 20 to replace the old pipes under the road. Workers will install new valves and clean the main tank during that time. Please store enough water in buckets and bottles for cooking, washing, and personal use before 9:00 A.M. The community tank behind the cultural house will be open all day for urgent needs, and volunteers will help elderly households carry water. We advise you not to run washing machines or water heaters while the supply is off to protect your equipment. After the repair, the water may look cloudy for a short time, so please let the tap run for two minutes first. We apologize for this disruption and thank you for your kind cooperation. For urgent support, call Mr. Hai at 555-0284.
