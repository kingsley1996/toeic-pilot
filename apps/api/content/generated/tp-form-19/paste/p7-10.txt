Old Street Weekend Fair: Shop, Eat and Play!
Come to the walking street this Saturday and Sunday, November 22-23, from 9:00 A.M. to 9:00 P.M. for our biggest fair of the year. More than eighty stalls will sell handmade bags, wooden toys, dried fruit, and second-hand books at friendly prices. The food corner behind the old temple offers noodle soup, grilled corn, and fresh sugarcane juice. Children can join free painting lessons at 10:00 A.M. and puppet shows at 3:00 P.M. each day. Visitors who spend over 200,000 dong will get a lucky coupon for the evening gift draw. Parking is free at the riverside lot, only five minutes on foot. Bring your family and friends to enjoy live music, tasty snacks, and happy shopping all weekend!
