Book Review: The Lantern on the Pier by Quynh Anh
New detective novel The Lantern on the Pier has become a favorite among weekend readers. [1] The story follows inspector Bao Lam as he returns to his fishing village to solve the disappearance of a lighthouse keeper. [2] Unlike many fast crime books, the writer spends the first eighty pages on village life, family meals, and childhood friends before the first real clue appears. [3] Critics praise the lively market scenes and the honest talks between the inspector and his aging mother. [4] The final forty pages move quickly, with two unexpected twists and a calm ending on the beach. The book has 320 pages and costs 145,000 dong at Fahasa stores. Readers who enjoy quiet observation more than car chases will love this gentle mystery. The author will sign copies at the district library at 9:00 A.M. on Sunday, November 9. Come early because seats are limited to eighty people.
