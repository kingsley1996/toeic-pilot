Rainbow Pool: Weekend Swimming Class for Children Ages 5 to 10
Our new weekend class helps children swim safely and have fun in the water. Lessons run every Saturday and Sunday from 8:00 A.M. to 9:30 A.M. at Rainbow Pool, 26 Le Loi Street, starting November 15. Each group has at most eight pupils with one trained coach, and the shallow training area is only one meter deep. The course fee is 800,000 dong for eight lessons, including a swim cap and goggles. All coaches hold lifesaving certificates and have at least three years of teaching kids. Parents can watch from the glass waiting room and enjoy free tea and wifi. Children should bring a towel, soap, and sandals. Register before November 10 at the front desk or call 555-0421 to keep a place, as only three groups open this winter.
