Ward Health Station Notice: Mosquito Spraying This Weekend
Dear residents of Block C, Our team will spray mosquito repellent in stairways, basements, and around the pond behind Block C from 7:00 A.M. to 10:00 A.M. on Saturday, November 8. The work aims to stop the spread of dengue fever after the rainy season. Please open your windows after 11:00 A.M. and wash food bowls before use. Keep small children and pets away from the yard during spraying and for two hours after. Fish tanks and beehives near the pond should be covered tightly that morning. If it rains heavily, the work will move to Sunday morning at the same time. Free larvicide bags will be given at the guard room from Friday afternoon. For questions about safety, call nurse Thao at 555-0369. Thank you for working with us to keep the neighborhood safe and clean.
