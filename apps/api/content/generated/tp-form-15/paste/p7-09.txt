Subject: Your health cover — three steps before the 20th

Dear staff, open enrolment for the 2026 clinic plan runs until Friday the 20th. After that date the plan locks and changes wait until January, except for life events like a marriage or a new baby, which reopen a thirty-day window for whoever they happen to.

Three steps: first, log into the portal with your badge number, not your email address, and open the "2026 compare" tab where the dental ceiling and the out-of-country excess are shown side by side with this year's. Second, pick one of the four tiers — Bronze covers GP visits only, Silver adds specialists, Gold adds dental, and Platinum carries the out-of-country excess to zero. Third, tick the box naming your dependants; unticked means single cover, and the system will not chase you for a decision.

If the portal shows the message "no tier on file" after the 20th, you defaulted to Bronze silently — the old plan does not roll forward this year. The benefits desk sits in Room 4 and answers the same questions all week, 9 to 5, but the queue is longest on Thursday, the day before the deadline.

[QUESTION]
What must staff do in the third step of enrolment?
(A) Upload a marriage or birth certificate
(B) Confirm whether anyone depends on their cover
(C) Choose the tier with zero excess
(D) Book a visit to the benefits desk in Room 4
Answer: B
Explanation: Bước ba là "tick the box naming your dependants" — xác nhận có người phụ thuộc hay không, với mặc định đơn nếu bỏ trống. | (A) "Upload a marriage or birth certificate" — giấy tờ đời tư chỉ mở cửa sổ đổi sau hạn, không là bước nào. | (B) "Confirm whether anyone depends on their cover" — đúng việc tick dependants ở bước ba. | (C) "zero excess" — chọn bậc nằm ở bước hai. | (D) "Book a visit to the benefits desk in Room 4" — bàn phúc lợi là nơi để hỏi, không phải bước bắt buộc.
