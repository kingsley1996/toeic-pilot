Member letter — the Winter Heritage run returns

Dear passenger, the Coastal Line's heritage steam excursion runs again on Saturday 6 December, hauling two carriages between Pier Junction and the quarries. Members may reserve by phone or at the counter; general sale opens only for seats the club leaves over.

Two changes from last year, both after passenger feedback. First, the carriage walk-through guide now speaks from the start of the run instead of the midpoint, so the bridge crossing is narrated as it happens. Second, the quiet carriage has moved from the rear to the lead position, which the catering cart cannot pass through — refreshments stay in carriage two all day, at counter prices.

Group bookings of ten travel free for the conductor, and school parties are welcome on the early departure, never the afternoon one, which turns around at the quarries before the museum stop.

Dear Ms. Vu, your phone booking is confirmed below. The quiet-carriage seat you requested exists only in the lead carriage, and that carriage seats eight in two facing rows of four — so your party of five has been split across two adjacent rows, which keeps everyone in the same compartment.

Tickets print at any staffed counter before nine on the day. The 09:40 departure sells out ahead of the 14:00 one every December; if your plans change, transfers are free until the Monday before, after which the fare is half-protected: the seat releases but the booking fee does not return.

We have logged your request for a table at the quarry cafe; it is held for twenty minutes past arrival, then released to the general list.
