Spotless Monthly — office cleaning that keeps its calendar

Spotless Monthly sends the same two-person crew to your office on a fixed weekly slot, and the invoice arrives monthly with the four visits itemised. Deep tasks — grout lines, high dusting, fridge clear-outs — rotate automatically: every second visit covers the pantry, every fourth visit the meeting rooms.

New clients choose one of three entry packages: Startup for one workroom, Studio for up to three rooms, or Floor for open plans to four hundred square metres. Supplies and disposal fees sit inside every package price, so no invoice has ever carried an add-on. Evening and weekend slots can be requested, though the fixed-slot promise only holds for weekday daytime calendars.

Crews change only when you do. Spotless keeps named staff on named buildings; if a cleaner leaves the rota, clients receive a two-week notice with the replacement's photo, not a locked door on Monday morning.

What can be inferred about Spotless invoices?
(A) They rise when deep tasks appear
(B) They arrive the day after every visit
(C) They list each cleaner's hours separately
(D) They never contain charges beyond the package
Answer: D
Explanation: "Supplies and disposal fees sit inside every package price, so no invoice has ever carried an add-on" — hóa đơn không bao giờ có khoản ngoài gói. | (A) "rise when deep tasks appear" — việc sâu xoay vòng định kỳ, không tính thêm tiền. | (B) "arrive the day after every visit" — hóa đơn về hằng tháng, bốn lượt gộp một kỳ. | (C) "each cleaner's hours" — chỉ có bốn visit được itemised, không có giờ công. | (D) "never contain charges beyond the package" — đúng kết luận của câu add-on.
