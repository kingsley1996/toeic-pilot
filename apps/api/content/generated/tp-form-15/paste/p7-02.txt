Warehouse Coordinator — Meridian Supply, District 7

Meridian Supply runs a 4,000-square-metre parts warehouse serving 120 clinics across the southern region. We are adding a coordinator to schedule inbound and outbound loads between five in the morning and seven in the evening.

Duties: build daily pick lists, verify incoming shipments against supplier notes, and run the Friday stock count with one assistant. Forklift certification is provided after hiring at company cost; applicants must already hold a category B licence and be comfortable with weekend rotation — roughly one Saturday in five.

Pay starts at 13.4 million dong a month plus a phone allowance; the allowance doubles for staff who also cover the Saturday rotation. Shortlisted candidates will be asked to lift a few crates during the interview; the physical check is deliberately short, and work boots are provided on the day.

[QUESTION]
What does the advertisement require applicants to have already?
(A) A forklift certificate
(B) A category B licence
(C) Work boots of their own
(D) Friday overtime experience
Answer: B
Explanation: Tin nêu "applicants must already hold a category B licence" — bằng lái B là điều kiện có sẵn, trong khi chứng chỉ nâng được công ty lo sau khi tuyển. | (A) "A forklift certificate" — chứng chỉ được đào tạo sau tuyển dụng, trả phí công ty. | (B) "A category B licence" — đúng thứ duy nhất bắt buộc có trước. | (C) "Work boots of their own" — giày được phát tại buổi phỏng vấn. | (D) "Friday overtime experience" — kiểm kho thứ Sáu là nhiệm vụ, không phải tiêu chí kinh nghiệm.
