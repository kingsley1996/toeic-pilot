Press release — Kettle brand launches the CupSoak travel mug

Kettle Houseware today launched the CupSoak travel mug nationwide, with the entire first production run already allocated to retailers before a single unit reached a shop shelf. The mug's inner capsule pops out for washing, a design Kettle calls the fastest clean-up in travel-drink history: "no brush, no corners," the launch note says.

Retail pricing is set at the higher end, and Kettle's own marketing brief to shops is explicit that CupSoak should sit beside premium grinders, not on the discount shelf. Company insiders hint a smaller, cheaper sibling model is "in the drawing stage, not the tooling stage" — an engineer's way of saying the plan is still on paper.

Letter from a dealer — Northside Kitchen Centre

To the Kettle distribution team,

We took twelve units of CupSoak for the window display and have now sold two. The walk-in crowd loves the pop-out capsule in the shop; at home, we hear the same sentence at the counter every week: it is heavier than the mug it replaces, and the lid clicks when you walk. The premium shelf placement you require has, honestly, made the comparison with the light aluminium mugs beside it unavoidable.

We are not asking for a return window. We would like a counter card listing the weight difference and the click, so customers are not surprised — and we would like that card before the December gift weeks, when travel mugs sell as fast as the launch run was promised to.
