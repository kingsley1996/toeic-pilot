Subject: Ten years of Friday mornings — a thank-you

Dear Ms. Ngo, when the tram line closed Lien's Coffee in 2016, you were one of eleven regulars who kept buying through the dusty weeks. Ten years on, the corner unit has two ovens, six tables inside, and a standing order of your oat latte every Friday.

As our decade of thanks, this letter carries a voucher code for ten free drinks, redeemable any weekday before the counter closes at eight in the evening. Please note the code on the receipt line below — we ask the holder to show this card, and only this card, at the till.

We are also moving Friday's bean tasting from noon to nine in the morning, "to catch the people the tram used to bring," founder Lien writes in the note pinned by the door. Regulars keep their seats; just nod at the chalkboard on your way in.

The team

[QUESTION]
What is the main purpose of the letter?
(A) To thank a customer with a gift
(B) To announce a change of owner
(C) To invite the holder to a new location
(D) To apologize for the counter closing early
Answer: A
Explanation: Thư mở bằng mốc mười năm với khách quen và "this letter carries a voucher code for ten free drinks" — món quà tri ân là trục chính. | (A) "To thank a customer with a gift" — đúng voucher mười ly tặng kèm lời cảm ơn. | (B) "change of owner" — người sáng lập Lien vẫn điều hành. | (C) "a new location" — mặt bằng góc chỉ là chuyện phát triển, không phải chuyển đi. | (D) "closing early" — tám giờ tối là lịch đóng quầy bình thường.
