Notice — Basement 2 parking closure, 6 to 10 October

Basement 2 closes for fire-sprinkler recertification from Monday 6 October through Friday 10 October, the entire day each morning to midnight. Vehicles still parked after six o'clock Sunday evening will be relocated at the owner's cost to the licensed impound yard on Nguyen Luong Bang street, where retrieval runs three hundred thousand dong a day.

Forty free temporary bays have been painted on the east lawn, open from half past six, first-come with no reservations. Once the lawn fills, attendants will direct arriving cars to the contract lot at Riverbank Mall, where building residents present this notice for half off the hourly rate between seven and nineteen.

Works run in two zones so at least one stair tower stays inspection-open at all times; the tower's recertification will therefore finish inside the five-day window, management promises. The bicycle racks outside Basement 2 remain in service throughout.

[QUESTION]
Why is the notice being posted?
(A) To announce towing rates at a city yard
(B) To arrange a temporary closure of a parking level
(C) To reserve lawn bays for residents only
(D) To advertise inspections at Riverbank Mall
Answer: B
Explanation: Thông báo mở đầu bằng việc đóng tầng hầm hai cả tuần để kiểm định vòi cứu hỏa — mục đích là báo trước quãng đóng tạm. | (A) "towing rates" — mức phí bãi gửi chỉ là cảnh báo kèm theo. | (B) "a temporary closure of a parking level" — đúng sự kiện được thông báo. | (C) "lawn bays for residents only" — sân east lawn mở first-come, không dành riêng. | (D) "inspections at Riverbank Mall" — trung tâm mua bán chỉ là bãi hợp đồng dự phòng.
