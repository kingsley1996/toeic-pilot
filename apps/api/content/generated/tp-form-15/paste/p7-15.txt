Internal note — the spreadsheet tool decision

As of January, the current free spreadsheet tool stops syncing with the archive server, which every finance report depends on. Finance has asked each department to send one delegate to the Friday walkthrough, where three paid suites — Ledger Pro, SheetOne and CalcBase — will be compared on live data rather than sales slides.

Two things the walkthrough will NOT do: decide the licence count, which stays with the department heads until the budget round in March, and publish pricing, since the suites quote different rates for ten-user and thirty-user bundles. Bring the question of file ownership to the session, though — export terms are where these tools differ most and where last year's migration burned us.

[PASSAGE]
Email from a delegate — reply to all finance staff

Hi everyone,

The walkthrough was useful, and I came away with three takeaways. SheetOne is the only suite whose formula language our archive macros survive unchanged in; both other tools rewrite them at import and lose the audit trail.

CalcBase has the lowest headline fee but charges per export beyond a monthly ceiling, and the archive pulls exports twice a week without fail — that ceiling is gone by the second Thursday. Ledger Pro reads our server directly and includes unlimited exports in the bundle shown on the table.

Licence count and the March decision are still department-head territory, as the note said; the delegate group can only recommend.
