Career Bridge Fair returns to the West Exhibition Hall

After two sold-out editions, the Career Bridge Fair returns next Wednesday to the West Exhibition Hall with eighty-one employers, the largest turnout since the fair began. [1] Registration opens at eight-thirty and the doors take walk-ins, though last year's arrival of six hundred people between nine and ten meant the noon coffee stations ran dry. [2]

This year's additions include a trades pavilion at the east end of the hall — plumbing, electrical and welding employers sharing a live demo ring — and, for the first time, a fifteen-minute "salary conversation" clinic where volunteers from two accounting firms review an offer letter with you on the spot. [3] Organisers stress that clinic slots are walk-up only and will close once forty conversations are booked.

Students without a printed CV can use the copy desk near the information booth for a small fee, or have one checked aloud by a career adviser in the quiet room behind hall control. [4] The fair closes at four; the evening bus loop on Circular 3 doubles its trips from four-fifteen.
