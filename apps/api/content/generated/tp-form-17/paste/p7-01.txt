Green Commute: Carpool Program Expands

More than 120 employees have joined our carpool program since March, easing the morning parking shortage at the Northgate site. The program groups staff who live in the same district so they can share fuel costs and take turns driving. Managers say fewer solo cars have also cut traffic jams at the front gate during rush hour.

To join, please finalize the registration form on the staff portal by Friday, May 16. New members must attend a fifteen-minute safety briefing in Room 101 before their first shared trip. Briefing sessions run every Tuesday and Thursday at 4:30 P.M. throughout May.

Carpool groups with three or more riders receive priority parking near the main entrance and monthly fuel vouchers worth twenty dollars per driver. Solo drivers will park in the distant east lot starting in June. A mobile app showing live seat availability will be released to members next quarter. Questions about routes or schedules? Contact Mr. Alan Reese at extension 308.
