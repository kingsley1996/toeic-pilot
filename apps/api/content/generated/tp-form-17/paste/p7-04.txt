Dear Ms. Whitfield,

Thank you for being a loyal reader of Milton Books for over five years. As a token of our thanks, please accept the enclosed coupon worth fifteen dollars off any purchase above forty dollars before August 31.

We are also pleased to share the rollout of our weekend storytelling hour for children, held every Saturday at 10:00 A.M. on the second floor. Your daughter attended twice in April, and our staff would love to welcome her back.

Our store opens daily from 9:00 A.M. to 9:00 P.M. at 14 Park Lane. We look forward to your next visit.

Warm regards,
Peter Lawson, Store Manager

[QUESTION]
Why did Mr. Lawson write the letter?
(A) To complain about a late payment
(B) To thank a loyal customer and send a coupon
(C) To announce the store closing
(D) To apply for a weekend job
Answer: B
Explanation: Phải nối 2 chỗ: "Thank you for being a loyal reader" + "please accept the enclosed coupon worth fifteen dollars". | (A) "To complain about a late payment" — không khiếu nại nào trong thư. | (B) "To thank a loyal customer and send a coupon" — đúng: cảm ơn khách thân thiết kèm phiếu giảm giá. | (C) "To announce the store closing" — trái hẳn: thư mong khách quay lại. | (D) "To apply for a weekend job" — bẫy: giờ kể chuyện cuối tuần là dịch vụ cho trẻ, không phải tin tuyển dụng.

In the letter, the word "rollout" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to
(A) removal
(B) delay
(C) introduction
(D) review
