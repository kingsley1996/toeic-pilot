Quarterly Air-Conditioner Care for Offices

Is your office ready for the hot season? BrightCool Services offers quarterly cleaning plans for workplaces with at least ten air conditioners. Each visit covers filter washing, gas checks, drain clearing, and thermostat testing for every registered unit, including both wall and ceiling models. Our technicians visit in January, April, July, and October, and members receive free minor parts plus 24-hour priority for emergency repairs. Technicians also check remote controls and clean vents during every visit. Reports after each visit list completed tasks for office managers. The fee is $45 per unit per visit, with a minimum of ten units per office. To book a visit, please call before Friday of the first month of each quarter. One-time cleanings are also available, but they cost 30 percent more and do not include priority repairs or free parts. Call 555-0148 today.
