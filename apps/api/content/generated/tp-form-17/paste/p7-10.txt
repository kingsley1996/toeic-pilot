Riverside Weekend Farm Market Returns

The riverside market by the old ferry pier opens every Saturday from 8 A.M. to 1 P.M. starting June 7. [1] More than thirty local farms will sell fresh vegetables, fruit, honey, and flowers directly to visitors. [2] A cooking demonstration begins at 10 A.M. on the wooden stage, where two guest chefs prepare simple dishes with morning produce. [3] Entry is free, and all stalls accept bank cards in addition to cash. [4] Families can rest in the new children's corner with small tables, while free parking is available across the bridge. A short workshop on home gardening starts at eleven near the east gate, with seats limited to twenty people. Baskets can be borrowed with a small deposit at the information booth. Local bakers join the market twice a month with fresh bread and pastries. Vendors cover their own tables, so visitors should bring reusable bags. The market runs rain or shine on the covered walkway, and it is cancelled only during heavy storms. Last spring the event drew nearly five thousand shoppers each weekend. Dogs are welcome if kept on short leads. The market office answers questions by phone from Monday to Friday. The organizers plan a harvest festival in October with live music and twice the stalls.
