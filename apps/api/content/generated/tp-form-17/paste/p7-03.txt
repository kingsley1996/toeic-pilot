Pour & Shine Bar Academy
Evening Bartending Course for Restaurant Staff

Learn to mix forty classic cocktails in just six weeks! Our evening course meets every Monday and Wednesday from 6:30 P.M. to 9:00 P.M., starting June 4 at 22 Harbor Street. The fee of $180 covers ingredients, tools, and a printed recipe handbook. The academy has trained over 900 bartenders since 2015.

Instructor Carla Nunez, a national cocktail champion, leads small classes of at most twelve trainees, so each learner gets daily hands-on practice behind a real bar counter. Graduates receive a skills certificate and free job-placement advice for hotels and cruise ships. All practice tools, from shakers to glassware, are provided during lessons and stored in personal lockers overnight. Coffee and snacks are served during the break at no extra charge.

Register by May 28 to save twenty percent. Morning trial lessons are free on May 20 and May 21. Free parking is available behind the building after 5:00 P.M. Call 555-7640 or visit our front desk. Please note that trainees must be at least eighteen years old.
