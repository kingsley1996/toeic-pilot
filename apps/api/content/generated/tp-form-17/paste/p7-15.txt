Maple Street Reading Club - Invitation to New Members
Date: May 10
From: Anna Cole, Club Organizer

Dear Neighbors,

We invite you to join our new Reading Club for a season of stimulating discussions and friendly gatherings. This season focuses on mystery novels, starting with a first meetup on Saturday, June 7, at 10:00 A.M. in Room 3 of the community library.

We meet once a month, and each session explores one book chosen by vote. New members can borrow the first book free from the front desk. Light refreshments are served during the break. Please reply by May 25 if you wish to join. We look forward to reading with you!

Warmly,
Anna Cole

[PASSAGE]
Maple Street Reading Club - Meeting Guidelines

1. Members may borrow up to two books at a time for three weeks.
2. Members who join the mystery novel group must bring a notebook with three discussion questions to each meeting.
3. Please arrive ten minutes early; late arrivals after 10:15 A.M. wait until the break to enter.
4. Turn off mobile phones during discussions.
5. Cancellations must be sent by email one day before the meeting.

Thank you for keeping our meetings pleasant and on time!

When will the first meetup be held?

(B) May 25 at 10:00 A.M.
