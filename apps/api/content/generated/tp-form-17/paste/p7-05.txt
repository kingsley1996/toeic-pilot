[PASSAGE]
From: Administration Office
To: All Staff
Subject: Mandatory Password Update for the Internal Portal
Date: September 2

For system security, every employee must change the internal portal password between September 8 and September 12. Log in with your staff number, click "Security Settings", and create a password of at least ten characters mixing letters and numbers. The portal stays open during the lunch break throughout the update week.

The old portal address will stop working on September 15, so please update any saved bookmarks. If you forget your staff number, ask Ms. Fiona Grant in Room 305 before noon on weekdays. The IT desk does not reset passwords by phone, so in-person visits are required for locked accounts.

Staff on business trips may authorize a colleague in writing to collect a temporary code. Temporary codes expire after twenty-four hours and cannot be reused. After the update, the portal offers faster search and a new leave-request form. Thank you for keeping our data safe.

[QUESTION]
What must employees do between September 8 and September 12?
(A) Collect a temporary code in person
(B) Update their saved bookmarks
(C) Visit Room 305 before noon
(D) Change their portal password
Answer: D
Explanation: Bài nêu "must change the internal portal password between September 8 and September 12". | (A) "Collect a temporary code in person" — bẫy: chỉ cho nhân viên đi công tác, không phải tất cả. | (B) "Update their saved bookmarks" — bẫy đúng-nhưng-khác-mốc: cập nhật bookmark vì địa chỉ cũ dừng ngày 15/9. | (C) "Visit Room 305 before noon" — bẫy: chỉ dành cho ai quên mã nhân viên, không phải tất cả. | (D) "Change their portal password" — đúng việc phải làm trong khung 8–12/9.
