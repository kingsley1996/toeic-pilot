Notice: Fire Safety Training for Spring Charity Concert Volunteers
Date: February 20
From: Safety Office

All volunteers supporting the Spring Charity Concert on April 12 must attend one full training session. The concert hall requires trained ushers for every performance.

Attendance at one full session is mandatory for all ushers and ticket assistants.

Session A: Tuesday, March 4, 9:00 A.M. to 11:00 A.M., Room 201
Session B: Thursday, March 6, 2:00 P.M. to 4:00 P.M., Room 201

The training covers fire extinguisher use, evacuation routes, and crowd guidance. Attendance will be recorded at the door. Please bring your volunteer badge and arrive ten minutes early. Late arrivals after 9:10 A.M. for Session A or 2:10 P.M. for Session B cannot receive a completion stamp. If you miss both sessions, contact the Safety Office by March 7 to arrange makeup. Questions about parking or seating duties can be sent to the volunteer desk by March 1.
