From: Helen Marsh, Office Manager, Northgate Legal
To: Sunrise Catering
Subject: Poor lunch service on March 8
Date: March 10

Dear Mr. Coleman,

Our firm ordered sixty boxed lunches for our client conference on March 8, and the service fell far below the standard in your contract. Your van arrived ninety minutes late, so our guests waited hungry in the meeting hall. Two of our partners had flown in from Chicago for this event, which made the delay especially embarrassing. Worse, twelve vegetarian meals were missing entirely, leaving several clients with nothing to eat. The chicken in the remaining boxes was cold and dry. We paid $900 in advance for this order and expect a partial refund within two weeks. Unless this matter is resolved fairly, we will place our monthly orders with another supplier. Please respond by March 14.

Sincerely,
Helen Marsh

[PASSAGE]
From: David Coleman, Sunrise Catering
To: Helen Marsh
Subject: Our apology and compensation
Date: March 12

Dear Ms. Marsh,

Thank you for your letter of March 10, and please accept our sincere apology for the failed lunch delivery. Our delivery van broke down on the highway, which caused the ninety-minute delay you described. The twelve vegetarian meals were packed but left behind at our kitchen during the rush to reload, and our head chef has added a checklist so no special meal is ever left behind. To make up for this poor service, we will reimburse twenty-five percent of your payment this week, prepare twelve replacement vegetarian meals free of charge for your April seminar, and provide free dessert at that event. We have hired a second dispatcher to prevent future delays. I hope you will give us one more chance to serve your firm.

Best regards,
David Coleman

[QUESTION]
What went wrong with the delivery on March 8?
(A) It served cold dessert in the meeting hall
(B) It was sent to the wrong address
(C) It cost more than the agreed price
(D) It arrived an hour and a half behind schedule
Answer: D
Explanation: Thư khiếu nại ghi "Your van arrived ninety minutes late", tức xe đến trễ chín mươi phút. | (A) "It served cold dessert in the meeting hall" — sai: bữa March 8 chỉ có gà bị nguội, tráng miệng chỉ được hứa cho sự kiện sau. | (B) "It was sent to the wrong address" — thư không hề nhắc giao nhầm địa chỉ. | (C) "It cost more than the agreed price" — giá $900 trả trước đúng thỏa thuận, không ai tính thêm. | (D) "It arrived an hour and a half behind schedule" — đúng sự cố vừa dẫn.
