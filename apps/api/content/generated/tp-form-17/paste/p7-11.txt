From: training@harbor-services.com
To: All front-desk staff
Subject: Invitation: Customer Service Workshop on June 12
Date: May 28

Dear Team Members,

We are pleased to invite you to our full-day Customer Service Workshop on Thursday, June 12, from 9 A.M. to 4:30 P.M. The program includes four practical sessions: Listening Skills, Phone Etiquette, Service Recovery, and Feedback Workshop. Attendance is required for all front-desk employees hired after March, and a printed certificate will be given to everyone who completes the full day. Ms. Patel, our senior service coach with twelve years of counter experience, will lead the opening session, and printed handouts will be provided for every topic.

The morning program focuses on everyday contact with callers and visitors, while the afternoon program turns to harder cases such as complaints and damaged deliveries. Both afternoon sessions will meet in Room 305. Employees who earned a service certificate last spring may skip the morning program but must join both afternoon sessions. Please bring your staff photo ID for registration, and arrive ten minutes before your first session so we can check names at the door.

A hot lunch will be served at noon in the second-floor cafeteria for all registered participants. To streamline the check-in process, please confirm your attendance by replying to this e-mail no later than June 5. Late replies cannot be guaranteed a seat because each room holds only forty chairs. Buses from the north branch leave at 8:10 A.M. and return by 5:15 P.M. If you have questions, call the training desk at 555-0276.

We look forward to seeing you there.

Best regards,
Nora Ellis
Training Coordinator
