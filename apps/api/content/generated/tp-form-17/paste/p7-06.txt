Tom Becker [8:40 A.M.]
Hi Lisa, could we swap weekend shifts? My sister arrives this Saturday, and I promised to meet her at noon. I can cover your Sunday night route instead.

Lisa Myers [9:05 A.M.]
Saturday noon is my airport shuttle run, so I cannot trade the whole day. You can take my Sunday night route if you drive the morning market loop for me on Saturday.

Tom Becker [9:18 A.M.]
Deal. I will drive the Sunday night route. Could you leave the fare box at the office on Friday evening?

Lisa Myers [10:23 A.M.]
I can try. My airport run ends at 6:00 P.M., so I should reach the office by seven. Traffic is usually light at that hour.

[QUESTION]
What will Tom Becker do on Sunday?
(A) Meet his sister at noon on Saturday
(B) Drive the airport shuttle run
(C) Drive the Sunday night route
(D) Leave the fare box at the office
Answer: C
Explanation: Phải nối 2 chỗ: Lisa đề nghị "You can take my Sunday night route" (9:05) + Tom chốt "Deal. I will drive the Sunday night route" (9:18). | (A) "Meet his sister at noon on Saturday" — bẫy đúng-nhưng-trả-lời-câu-khác: đó là việc thứ Bảy, câu hỏi hỏi Chủ nhật. | (B) "Drive the airport shuttle run" — đó là ca của Lisa, không phải Tom. | (C) "Drive the Sunday night route" — đúng việc Tom nhận sau khi đổi ca. | (D) "Leave the fare box at the office" — đó là việc Lisa được nhờ, không phải Tom.

[QUESTION]
At 10:23 A.M., what does Ms. Myers mean when she writes, "I can try"?
(A) She asks Tom to drive the airport run
(B) She refuses to visit the office
(C) She has already delivered the fare box
(D) She will attempt to bring the fare box on Friday evening
Answer: D
Explanation: Câu quyết định nguyên văn: "I can try. My airport run ends at 6:00 P.M., so I should reach the office by seven. Traffic is usually light at that hour." | (A) "She asks Tom to drive the airport run" — ca sân bay vẫn của cô ấy, không nhờ Tom. | (B) "She refuses to visit the office" — trái hẳn: cô ấy đồng ý cố gắng, không từ chối. | (C) "She has already delivered the fare box" — bẫy sai thì: "should reach" là dự định, chưa làm xong. | (D) "She will attempt to bring the fare box on Friday evening" — đúng: nhận lời sẽ cố mang hộp vé tới văn phòng.
