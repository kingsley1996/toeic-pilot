Golden Crust Bakery Doubles Its Bread Line
By Staff Reporter

Our town's oldest bakery, Golden Crust, will double its bread line this spring. [1] The family firm bought two large ovens in January and will add a night shift starting March 10. [2] Twenty new workers will join the forty staff already at the King Street factory. [3] Owner Mr. Alan Price says the extra bread will supply schools and hotels across the county. [4] A new shop next to the factory will sell warm loaves every morning from 7:00 A.M.

The bakery has served the town for thirty years and still uses the founder's recipes. Job applications close on February 28, and training for new workers begins in the first week of March. Delivery trucks leave the King Street factory twice daily to keep shelves stocked.

[QUESTION]
According to the article, when will the night shift start?
(A) In January
(B) On March 10
(C) On February 28
(D) At 7:00 A.M. daily
Answer: B
Explanation: Bài ghi rõ ca đêm bắt đầu ("will add a night shift starting March 10"). | (A) "In January" — bẫy: đó là lúc mua lò nướng, không phải mở ca đêm. | (B) "On March 10" — đúng mốc vừa dẫn. | (C) "On February 28" — là hạn nộp đơn xin việc, không phải mở ca. | (D) "At 7:00 A.M. daily" — là giờ cửa hàng mới bán bánh, không phải ca đêm.
