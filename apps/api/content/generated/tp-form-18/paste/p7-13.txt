From: Ms. Karen Doyle, Bright Office Co.
To: SparkleClean Services
Subject: Poor cleaning service on March 3
Date: March 5

Dear Ms. Frost,

Your team cleaned our office on the evening of March 3, but the result was far below our contract. There was still dust on the desks, the bins on the second floor were full, and the restrooms had no soap or paper towels. Two of our meeting rooms were left unlocked overnight, which worries us greatly.

We pay $600 a month for daily cleaning and expect much better. Please send a supervisor to inspect our office at 9:00 A.M. on March 10 and fix every problem the same week. If nothing improves, we will end the contract in April.

Yours sincerely,
Karen Doyle

[PASSAGE]
From: Ms. Anna Frost, SparkleClean Services
To: Ms. Karen Doyle
Subject: Our apology and action plan
Date: March 7

Dear Ms. Doyle,

Thank you for your letter of March 5. Please accept our apology for the poor cleaning on March 3. The regular team leader was sick that evening, and a new helper missed several tasks on his checklist.

To rectify the problems, we will clean your whole office again free of charge on March 9, assign a supervisor to check the work every evening, and cut 15% off your April bill. I will also call you on March 10 after the inspection. We value your contract and hope to keep your trust.

Best regards,
Anna Frost

[QUESTION]
What can be inferred from the two letters?
(A) The client ended the cleaning contract in April
(B) The company admitted the fault and offered free remedies
(C) The March 10 inspection visit was cancelled
(D) Trash removal earned the worst survey score
Answer: B
Explanation: Thư thứ nhất chê bụi còn trên bàn ("There was still dust on the desks") còn thư thứ hai hứa dọn lại miễn phí ("we will clean your whole office again free of charge") nên công ty nhận lỗi và bù đắp. | (A) "The client ended the cleaning contract in April" — bẫy: hủy hợp đồng chỉ là lời dọa nếu không cải thiện. | (B) "The company admitted the fault and offered free remedies" — đúng điều suy ra từ hai thư vừa dẫn. | (C) "The March 10 inspection visit was cancelled" — ngược hẳn: thư hẹn gọi sau buổi kiểm tra March 10. | (D) "Trash removal earned the worst survey score" — bẫy đúng-nhưng-trả-lời-câu-khác: thấp nhất là Dusting 2.1, không phải Trash Removal.
