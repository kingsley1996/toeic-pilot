Blue Anchor Seafood — A New Star by the River
Posted by food lover Dana Kim, September 14

My family tried Blue Anchor, the new seafood restaurant by the river ferry pier, last Sunday. The grilled shrimp with garlic butter was superb, and the fish soup came in huge bowls at only $12 each. Our table on the open deck faced the sunset, and the kids loved watching the boats pass by.

Service was quick although the hall was full, and the staff even gave us a free fruit plate for waiting ten minutes. The menu is rich, but the crab dishes are pricey at over $40. We will surely return next month with my parents. Parking is easy in the big lot across the street.

Answer: B
