Store Shift Changes — Weekend Cover
Anna Cole (store manager) [Friday 4:02 P.M.]
Hi Ben, can you cover the Saturday morning shift? Our cashier Mia is sick, and the store opens at 8:00 A.M. I can take your Sunday afternoon shift in return.
Ben Harris [Friday 4:20 P.M.]
Saturday morning is difficult because my bus arrives at 9:30. I can work from 10:00 A.M. to the end of the shift at 1:00 P.M. if someone opens the store.
Anna Cole [Friday 4:35 P.M.]
I can try. I will open the store myself at 8:00 A.M. and stay until you arrive at ten.
Ben Harris [Friday 4:41 P.M.]
Deal. I will close the Sunday salad bar early so we both leave on time.
Ben Harris [Friday 4:47 P.M.]
Thanks, Anna. I will bring the new price tags on Saturday and stack them on the morning shelves before customers arrive.
Anna Cole [Friday 4:52 P.M.]
Perfect. I will confirm the weekend roster tonight and send you both a copy.

[QUESTION]
What are the speakers discussing?
(A) Ordering new salad bar supplies for Sunday
(B) Arranging cover for the Saturday morning shift
(C) Hiring a full-time cashier for the store
(D) Closing the store for the whole weekend
Answer: B
Explanation: Anna mở đầu nhờ trực ca sáng thứ Bảy ("can you cover the Saturday morning shift") rồi hai người chia ca đón khách. | (A) "Ordering new salad bar supplies for Sunday" — quầy salad chỉ được nhắc lúc đóng sớm, không ai đặt hàng. | (B) "Arranging cover for the Saturday morning shift" — đúng việc cả hai đang bàn. | (C) "Hiring a full-time cashier for the store" — Mia chỉ ốm tạm thời, không ai tuyển người. | (D) "Closing the store for the whole weekend" — ngược hẳn: cả hai cố giữ cửa hàng mở cửa.
