HomeStyle Furniture Needs Weekend Sales Staff
HomeStyle Furniture at 88 Lake Avenue is hiring two part-time sales assistants for weekends. Work hours are Saturday and Sunday from 10:00 A.M. to 6:00 P.M., with a one-hour lunch break. Pay is $14 per hour, plus a bonus for high sales.
No experience is needed because full training is given in the first week. Applicants must be friendly, speak clearly, and be able to lift light boxes. Students are welcome to apply. Send your resume to jobs@homestyle.com before October 5. Interviews will be held on October 8, and the job starts on October 12.

Explanation: Bài ghi rõ giờ cuối tuần ("Work hours are Saturday and Sunday from 10:00 A.M. to 6:00 P.M."). | (A) "From 10:00 A.M. to 6:00 P.M." — đúng giờ vừa dẫn. | (B) "From 8:00 A.M. to 10:00 P.M." — khung giờ này không xuất hiện trong bài. | (C) "Every weekday evening after school" — ngược hẳn: việc chỉ làm thứ Bảy và Chủ nhật. | (D) "Only on Sunday mornings" — bẫy thiếu: làm cả hai ngày cuối tuần.
