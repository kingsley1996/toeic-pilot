PowerZone Gym Opens Downtown — Work Out Any Time
PowerZone Fitness will open a 24-hour gym at 45 River Street on November 1. Members can use modern cardio machines, free weights, and two group-exercise studios at any hour, including weekends and holidays. Trained coaches stay on the floor from 6:00 A.M. to 10:00 P.M. every day.
The first-month membership costs only $25, half the usual price, and includes a free fitness test plus one personal training session. Visitors can try all equipment free during the open house on October 25 and 26. To join, visit the front desk with a photo ID or call 555-0271. Student discounts and family plans are offered all year round.
